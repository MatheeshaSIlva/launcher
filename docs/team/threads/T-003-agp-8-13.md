## 1. Proposal and split (lead; a small change: protocol §2's short form)

**Goal.** The build runs on AGP 8.13.2 and Gradle 8.13 with everything else as it is (Kotlin 2.0.21, Compose 1.7.5,
compileSdk 35, JDK 17), so T-002 then changes only Kotlin and Compose.

**Approach.** Root `build.gradle.kts`: `com.android.application` and `com.android.library` 8.7.3 → 8.13.2. Gradle wrapper
8.9 → 8.13 (AGP 8.13's minimum). Whatever the new AGP or its lint refuses, fixed and listed. Known before starting, from
T-002's test of lint 8.13.2: three real old bugs it reports (`NewApi`: `WindowInsetsController.setSystemBarsBehavior`, an
Android 11 API, called without a version check at `TurnCover.kt:70`, `Shade.kt:348`, `BarsTestActivity.kt:93`, minSdk
29: on Android 10 each throws), fixed with `Build.VERSION.SDK_INT >= 30` guards, which changes behaviour on Android 10
only; and `PropertyEscape` on `local.properties` (the machine's own git-ignored file; its escaping is fixed locally, CI
has none).

**Declared class-list diff, written before reading the build's dex** (against the current `engine` build):

| Category | Expected | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Landroidx/**`, `Lkotlin/**`, `Lkotlinx/**` | **no change** | no library version moves in this slice |
| `Ldev/launcher/**` synthetic classes and members (`$$ExternalSyntheticLambdaN`, `$r8$lambda$…`, `$$ExternalSyntheticApiModelOutline…`, backported-method helpers) | renumbered, added or removed | D8 moves with AGP and synthesises differently |
| `Ldev/launcher/**` named classes and their named members | **no change**, except the three guarded call sites' enclosing methods (same counts, different bodies: invisible to the listing) | anything else stops the slice until explained |
| merged manifest | **no change** | anything else is named and explained |
| `baselineProfiles/0/app-debug.dm` | still produced at that path, non-empty | CI and `tools/device.sh` read it there; CI silently ships the APK alone if it is missing |

**Verification.** `scripts/check.sh` green (lint now 8.13.2: its new findings either fixed as above or named); the dex
diff sorted into the categories above with every name change listed; the `.dm` checked and `pm art dump` on the emulator
shows `speed-profile [reason=install-dm]`; `tools/smoke_emulator.sh` passes. The S24 comparison runs once, after T-002,
on reference `engine` against T-002's final build (both slices together); if it shows a regression, a T-003-only build
is measured to tell the two apart.

**Split.** claude: everything (two build lines, the three guards, the measurements). Codex: the cross-check in §6.
