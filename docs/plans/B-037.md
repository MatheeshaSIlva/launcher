# B-037: iOS 27's Notification Center comes down as a sheet of glass

Research, 2026-10-25 (our iOS 27 Simulator captures, read by the architect and checked by the orchestrator; a web search of
Apple's material and reviews). No plan yet: the owner chooses the scope first.

## What iOS 27 does (our captures)
Notification Center, slow pull (`ios-reference/out/pass5/test31_ncHoldRelease/f1/f0084-f0110.png`, ~22.16-23.0 s):
- The sheet has a real leading edge: rounded bottom corners (about the screen's radius), a thin bright rim along it and up
  its sides, the home indicator riding on it ~10 pt above the edge.
- The edge band is see-through and bends what is behind: home's dock and its icons stay visible through the bottom 15-30 pt
  of the sheet, squeezed (Safari and Messages as ~12 pt slivers against 65 pt): a lens, not a cut.
- The sheet starts clear: home's icons and widgets show through its upper part; the lock clock arrives as glass numerals
  refracting home (the "9" takes the Maps widget's green); the wallpaper then fills in from below along a wavy line that
  follows the wallpaper's own shapes, gone within ~10 frames. (Maybe the default wallpaper's own lock/home animation:
  unconfirmed.)
- The wallpaper stays still while the sheet moves (`docs/IOS27_MOTION.md:46-49`); the edge bends towards the finger
  (documented there, not measured).
- Home under the sheet is dimmed, not blurred or scaled.
Control Center: no sheet and no edge; the whole screen blurs and dims within ~3 frames; no lens seen. Ours already matches
it in kind (its ramp is slower on purpose).

## What the web says
Nothing published describes the pull-down's edge. Several reviews confirm the lock-screen sheet's edge distorts ("lensing")
when swiped up (Six Colors' iOS 26 review; Apple Community: Reduce Motion removes it), and one user saw home stay visible
behind a slowly pulled Notification Center until release. iOS 27 beta 3 reportedly made "foreground elements appear first"
on the pull (one outlet). Apple's WWDC25 "Meet Liquid Glass": lensing is the material's defining trait; thicker glass bends
more; highlights follow the geometry. Third-party reconstructions: displacement by a convex bezel profile near the edge.

## What ours does
`NotificationCenterView` is one opaque layer moved by `translationY`: the wallpaper slides with it, its bottom is a straight
cut (no corners, rim, indicator or lens), nothing under it is dimmed, its clock and platters sample only the wallpaper
(`Shade.backgroundOf` returns null for NC: no picture of what is behind is taken).

## Options
- A: the glass edge. A lens band (~30-40 pt, fixed size, moved) drawn by `MaterialPainter` over our sharp picture of what is
  behind (home's recorded picture, an app's kept snapshot, the wallpaper on the lock screen), rounded bottom corners, rim,
  the home indicator on the sheet, the wallpaper still while the sheet moves, home dimmed under it. Not affected by Samsung's
  blur (no live blur under NC): the S24 and the emulator behave alike. Risk: an app's snapshot can arrive late (the rim alone
  first, the lens materialising when it comes); GPU cost of a band redrawn every frame (measured with framestats).
- B: A without the lens (corners, rim, indicator, still wallpaper, dimming). Cheapest; loses the refraction.
- C: A, then the whole sheet starts clear: what is behind seen through it, the clock as glass over it, the wallpaper filling
  in. Larger and riskier at 120 Hz; worth it only if the fill-in is the sheet's, not the wallpaper's.

## Owner's answers (2026-10-25)
- Scope: the glass edge (A), then the clear sheet (C), in two steps, each checked on the S24.
- References first: one CI capture run (a slow pull with a still photo wallpaper, over home and over an app, with holds)
  before building.

## Capture pass 10 (2026-10-25, run 40167905254; frames `ios-reference/out/pass10/test81_ncGlassHome`, `test82_ncGlassApp`)
The photo wallpaper could not be set (iOS 27's Simulator Settings has no Wallpaper row): the default wallpaper, but while
the finger holds the sheet no wallpaper shows at all, so this does not hide the answers.
- While pulled and held, the whole sheet is clear glass: home or the app shows through unchanged (1-10 % darker), no
  wallpaper, clock, date or home indicator. Over home the status bar goes at the first frame; over an app it stays.
- The edge follows the finger 1:1 and stays a straight horizontal line: no bend toward the finger (finger at x 70, 201,
  352 differ by under 1 grey level; `IOS27_MOTION.md`'s "bends towards the finger" is wrong).
- Bottom corners round, ~50-55 pt (by eye), bending what is in them strongly.
- A ~1 pt rim, tinted (tan over dark, yellow-olive over white), with red/blue chromatic fringes; a blue/purple fringe up
  both screen sides for the sheet's height.
- Bottom lens band ~26 pt: what lies ~15-20 pt above is stretched down into it (magnified smear, not squeezed); its last
  3-5 pt are a flattened mirror copy of content ~25-30 pt above with cyan/magenta separation ("General" flipped).
- Side lens ~15 pt from each screen edge, stretching content out to the edge.
- Below the edge, what is behind is dimmed by a uniform 30 % black (no gradient), ramping in over ~0.4 s at the pull's
  start over an app (~60 ms over home), clearing in ~0.15-0.2 s on a fall-back.
- Released from a standstill at 50 % it falls back closed; at 75 % it opens.
- On commit (opening): over home the icons inside the sheet zoom up (~1.24x then ~2x) and fade within ~60 ms while the dock
  below stays; the lock wallpaper, "Carrier", status bar and home indicator (~10 pt above the bottom) appear at once; the
  clock arrives as glass numerals near the top and drops ~60 pt into place over ~150 ms, the date fading in after.
- Not shown: a still wallpaper's fill-in, fast pulls, dark mode, exact radius, 120 Hz timing.
So the clear sheet (C) is the pull itself: while the finger holds it, Notification Center is a clear glass slab with lens
edges over what is behind; its own content (wallpaper, clock, notifications) arrives on commit.
