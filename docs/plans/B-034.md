# Plan: B-034, the Pixel shade's groups and expanded notifications as Android 17 shows them

Architect's plan (2026-10-24), verdict READY. Owner's answer: an opened group shows at most 8 children (D-026). Decisions:
D-027. Bug: B-034 in `docs/ROADMAP.md`. QA's reproduction: `tools/shots/b034_*.png` (`b034_cmp.png`: ours opened, ours
after a child's chevron, stock opened, stock after a child's chevron).

## Findings that shape the plan
- `place()` draws with `row.height`, which takes its new value at once: expanding any card already pops its height and
  content; only the row's `y` moves (`motion.nc.reflow`). Slices 1-2 do not make this worse; slice 3 fixes it for all.
- A row whose key comes back while still fading out is `y.snapTo(r.top)` (line ~187): reopening a group mid-close jumps.
- `drawChip` points up only for `row.kind == CHILD && row.first`.
- `Anim.height` is written but never read.
- A group card's key is `pkg|groupKey`, a child's its item key: a group falling to one item turns its last child into a
  plain card without a jump. The model keeps this; the header row takes the group card's key (the same Anim as the
  collapsed GROUP row).
- Pixel cards move on Notification Center's roles (`nc.reflow`, `nc.appear`, `nc.leave`, `nc.swipe-*`): kept.
- `Notifs.Item` holds Android types and unit tests have no Robolectric: the pure model works on keys only.

## Scope
Do: a pure row model with a pure toggle and JVM tests; an opened group is one card (a header row, then at most 8
children); a child's chevron expands or collapses that child only; the header's chevron or body closes the group;
children have no icon; a collapsed child shows "Title • time", an expanded child "time" on its first line (no app name);
hairline dividers inside an opened group, corners only at its outer edges, no gaps inside; a collapsed group shows its
header then up to 2 children one line each "Title Text" (5 for the first card in the list); every change animated
(height, content crossfade, children unfolding out of the collapsed lines and folding back, the chevron turning), all
interruptible.
Do NOT: stock's notification shelf; B-035 (the press highlight colour) beyond keeping ripples working; `motion.qs.*` roles
for cards; Android's per-element TransformState (we crossfade); swipe-to-reveal or snooze; replying in place (B-001); the
iOS Notification Center (`shade/nc/*`); 2/5/8 as layout options.

## Slice 1: the row model, children that expand, a header that closes (functional fix; can ship alone)
0. On the emulator's own shade (our accessibility service off): open a group, expand a child, close the group with the
   header chevron, reopen it. Is the child still expanded? Note it in the test's comment and in
   `docs/SHADE_PIXEL_EVALUATION.md`, and copy it in the toggle; if it cannot be read, forget the child's expansion when its
   group closes.
1. New `app/src/main/java/dev/launcher/app/shade/qs/NotifRows.kt` (internal, no `android.*` imports, ~120 lines):
   - Types (moved out of CardsBlock): `Section { ONGOING, ALERTING, SILENT }`, `RowKind { CARD, GROUP, HEADER, CHILD,
     HEADING, FOOTER }`, `Corner { BIG, JOINED, NONE }`, `Gap { NONE, JOINED, SECTION, FOOTER }`,
     `Chevron { NONE, UP, DOWN }`, `Head { APP_TIME, TIME, NONE }` (what the first line names).
   - `CardIn(key, section, itemKeys)` (items latest first).
   - `RowModel(key, kind, section, cardKey, index, expanded, lines, count, icon, head, chevron, top: Corner,
     bottom: Corner, gapBefore: Gap, divider)`: `index` into the card's items; `lines` is the GROUP's one-line count.
   - `ExpandState(opened, expandedChildren, closedFirst)`: mutable sets of keys.
   - `NotifRows.build(cards, state, footer): List<RowModel>`, `NotifRows.toggle(row, state)`.
   - Constants with their AOSP source (NotificationChildrenContainer): `LINES_COLLAPSED = 2`, `LINES_FIRST = 5`,
     `MAX_CHILDREN = 8`.
   - Rules: a single card is expanded when (first card and not in `closedFirst`) or in `opened` (today's rule). A group
     not in `opened`: one GROUP row, `lines = min(n, 5 if first card else 2)`, a count, a DOWN chevron. An opened group:
     a HEADER row (key = the card key, icon, APP_TIME, count, UP chevron), then `min(n, 8)` CHILD rows (key = its item
     key, no icon, `expanded = key in expandedChildren`, head TIME when expanded and NONE when collapsed (the title line
     carries " • time"), UP or DOWN by expansion, a divider). Gaps: NONE inside an opened group, JOINED between cards of a
     section, SECTION before a heading or a new section, FOOTER before the footer. Corners: a card's outer edge BIG at its
     section column's ends and JOINED where cards meet; NONE inside an opened group. Toggle: CARD as today (lines
     ~727-731); GROUP adds to `opened`; HEADER removes from `opened` and applies step 0's rule to its children's keys;
     CHILD flips its key in `expandedChildren`, the group stays open.
2. `CardsBlock.kt`: drop `Kind`, `Section` and the private Card/Row structure (`Row` wraps a `RowModel` plus `top` and
   `height`, its `Notifs.Item` is `card.items[index]`); `relayout` calls `NotifRows.build`; heights: HEADER `pad*2 + icon`
   (72 dp, as measured), CHILD as a card minus the icon column (text still from `textX`), GROUP as now until slice 2;
   gaps from `gapBefore` (NONE = 0); `toggle()` calls `NotifRows.toggle`; `drawChip` takes `count` and `chevron` from the
   model (the `row.first` rule goes); `drawCard` draws the icon only when `icon`, the first line from `head` (APP_TIME
   "App • time" from the lead; TIME the time alone), corner radii from `top`/`bottom` (NONE = 0); `itemsOf`: a HEADER
   gives all the card's items (also past the 8); a CHILD's drawn x is its own swipe plus its header's, so an open group
   swipes from its header; `clear()` skips CHILD rows whose header is being cleared (the group flies out as one card);
   taps: a HEADER's body toggles (closes), a CHILD's body opens its app; `closed()` also clears `expandedChildren`.
3. New test `app/src/test/java/dev/launcher/app/shade/qs/NotifRowsTest.kt`.

## Slice 2: one card, its dividers, the collapsed group's lines (the look)

   `tools/design/build_ios27_theme.py` (the divider an alias of an existing separator or component token, judged; sizes

   other appearances (then TextKeys `comp.qs.card.line-title`, `comp.qs.card.line-text`).
5. `CardsBlock`: GROUP height `pad + app line + group-lines-top + lines * group-line + pad` (adjusted to stock); GROUP

   one ellipsized line each; a CHILD with `divider` draws a hairline of `divider-width` at its top, across what the
   screenshot shows (text column or card edge), in `comp.qs.card.divider`, faded with the row.

## Slice 3: motion (nothing pops, interruptible)
6. `Anim` gains `h` (MotionValue, scale 2, as `y`) and `k` (0..1, how expanded or opened). `relayout` animates `h` and `k`
   on `motion.nc.reflow`, same role and frame as `y`; `place()` uses `a.h.value`; `contentHeight` and the scroll bounds
   stay on targets.
7. Drawing: the surface once per card, from its first row's animated top to the largest animated bottom of its rows
   (leaving rows included); a row with its own non-zero swipe is left out and draws its own segment with JOINED corners.
   Ripple per row with its radii. Each row's content clipped to its animated rect (rect clip). Collapsed content at
   `1-k`, expanded at `k`; a GROUP/HEADER's one-line rows fade out as `k` goes to 1; the chevron turns with `k`. The
   expanded `StaticLayout` cached per (item key, postTime, width).
8. Opening and closing: a new CHILD whose group was a GROUP row starts at its line's place
   (`header top + lines-top + i*group-line`, height `group-line`); children past the shown lines start at the old card's
   bottom; they move on `nc.reflow` and fade in on `nc.appear`. Closing: leaving children animate `y` and `h` back to those
   places on `nc.reflow`, fading on `nc.leave`; the header's `h` grows back and its lines fade in. A key coming back while
   leaving keeps its `y`, `h` and alpha and animates on (never `snapTo`). Start and end places in a pure
   `NotifRows.foldLine(index, lines)` (line index or "below"), unit-tested.
9. Panel open (`animate = false`) snaps everything, as today.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1 (s1) Opening a group (chip or body) gives a header row: app icon, "App • time", the count chip with an UP chevron;
  its key is the group card's.
- AC2 (s1) A child's chevron expands that child only; the group stays open; other children keep their state.
- AC3 (s1) An expanded child's chevron collapses only it.
- AC4 (s1) The header's chevron or body closes the group into its collapsed card.
- AC5 (s1) Children draw no icon (text at `textX`); collapsed: "Title • time", text, DOWN chevron; expanded: the time alone
  on the first line, the title, the whole text, its actions, UP chevron.
- AC6 (s1+s2) An opened group is one card: no gaps between its rows, BIG/JOINED corners only at its outer top and bottom,
  NONE inside, a hairline above each child (s2).
- AC7 (model s1, drawing s2) A collapsed group lists its latest `min(n, N)` children one line each "Title Text"; N = 5 for
  the first card in the list, 2 for the others.
- AC8 (s1) An opened group shows at most 8 children; the count stays the total; swiping the header clears all.
- AC9 (all) Unchanged: a single card's look and chevron, the first card expanded by default and `closedFirst`, swipe to
  dismiss, Clear all's stagger, the Silent heading's clear, the footer buttons, ripples, scrolling to the end with and
  without the media player, the panel closing to a reset state.
- AC10 (s3) Expanding/collapsing any card or child, opening/closing a group: no row's top, height, content or chevron
  changes in one frame (slowed frames show in-between values).
- AC11 (s3) A second tap mid-animation reverses from where things are; reopening a group mid-close does not snap children.
- AC12 (s3) No seam or overlap between rows of an opened group at any frame, Pixel (opaque) and iOS 27 (glass), light and
  dark.

## Regression areas (all `shade/qs/CardsBlock.kt`)
Swipe (`beginSwipe`, `swipeBy`, `endSwipe`, `itemsOf`): the header carries its children, a child swipes alone, a
non-clearable item makes the header resist. Clear all and the Silent clear: children of a cleared header skipped; delays
still cap at 6 steps. Taps (`tap`, `chips`, `actionHits`): an expanded child's actions still hit. Ripple per row radii.
Scrolling (`bounds`, `visibleHeight`, `contentHeight`): a group of 8 opening; a shrinking list while scrolled. Media
(`scrimRestTop`). Landscape split (`cardsWidth`). Lock screen redaction: the one-line text must not show what the card
hides. The first card rule and `closedFirst`. `relayout`'s leaving logic (s3). New notifications arriving in an opened
group. The iOS 27 theme under the Pixel layout (glass union surface in s3 must not double-draw). Performance: no
allocation per frame (cache layouts).
