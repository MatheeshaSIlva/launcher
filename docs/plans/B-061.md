## What the golden shows (measured, not by eye)

(`tools/shots/fidelity/baseline2-canon-20261027-040014/capture/ours/`), both drawing Roboto (`--font system`). Metric: the
median width of horizontal ink runs (stem width), independent of the words; ink total and box as a cross-check.

Compose text falls back to the default family at the asked weight (Roboto is variable on Android 17): representative of a
Pixel, and it matches. View text whose weight comes from a family name falls back to Roboto Regular on the emulator: not
representative. The carrier is View text: the theme follows its Android 17 style chain, never the emulator's picture.
A regular family with no weight attribute: a Pixel draws 400, ours at 500 is a theme error (change it). A medium family
(`google-sans-medium`, `config_headlineFontFamilyMedium`, a `variable-*` emphasized name): a Pixel draws 500, the gap is an
emulator artefact (keep 500, record it).

## Steps

1. Source read (emulator, adb reads only): pull SystemUI's APK, `aapt2 dump xmltree` / `dump resources`: the layout holding
   `mobile_carrier_text`, its `textAppearance` and parents to `fontFamily`, `textFontWeight`, `textStyle`;
   `config_headlineFontFamily(*)` in the Pixel overlay. Write the chain into `docs/SHADE_PIXEL_EVALUATION.md` (a "B-061" note
   after stage 8); decide 400 or 500 by the rule above.
2. `tools/fidelity/stems.py` (two PNGs and named boxes, or a JSON of boxes; median stem px per box and side) and
   `tools/fidelity/test_stems.py` (synthetic bars, both polarities). About 120 lines.
3. Only if step 1 says regular: `tools/design/build_pixel_theme.py`, `comp.qs.header.carrier` to weight 400 with the
   style's tracking and `src` naming the resolved chain; fix the `src` of `comp.qs.header.date` and `.clock` to cite step 1's
   resources and the stems. Regenerate `pixel.json` with the builder (never by hand); its diff touches only those tokens.
   If medium: no theme change.
4. `scripts/check.sh`, `DEVICE=emulator-5554 tools/reinstall.sh` (APK md5 checked), `tools/fidelity/baseline.sh --out DIR`,
   `stems.py` on the boxes below against the golden.
5. Docs: FIDELITY_BASELINE (the stem table, the corrected mask statement, the re-run's 8 rows as "baseline 2, B-061
   re-run"); ROADMAP B-061 status; DECISIONS D-045; the quick-row time finding into B-053's line.
