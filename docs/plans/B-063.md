# Plan: B-063, record the emulator's flash units in every capture and gate the baseline on them

Roadmap item: bug B-063 (MF fidelity harness). Track: quick (harness only, no app code). Architect (Opus), 2026-10-28.
Verdict: READY. Owner's answer to its question (2026-10-28): the app's torch follows Android's back camera only, as a
separate fix after this one (B-065); not part of this plan.

## Finding

`dumpsys media.camera` on today's emulator (build CP41.260828.004.A7): two cameras, `device@1.1/internal/1` "Has a flash
unit: true", "Facing: Front"; `device@1.1/internal/10` "Has a flash unit: false", "Facing: Back". Our `Controls.kt` ~346
takes any camera with `FLASH_INFO_AVAILABLE` (so ours shows the Flashlight tile on this boot); AOSP's FlashlightController
takes the back camera only (stock shows it unavailable on both boots). Baseline 2's boot had no camera with a flash; the
golden (stock) and baseline 2's ours capture are the same boot (both place it at 18:57:28Z).

## Scope

Do:
- `torch_state` in `tools/fidelity/capture.sh`: reads `dumpsys media.camera` (no side effect), pairs each `Has a flash
  unit: X` with the next `^ *Facing: Y`; prints the sorted, comma-joined lowercase facings with a flash (`front`, `back`,
  `back,front`, `external`...), `none` when cameras are listed and none has a flash (also "Number of camera devices: 0"),
  `unknown` when the dump has no such lines or a flash line has no facing.
- capture.sh (both sides): read once in `apply_setup`, record `"torch"` in the manifest's `setup`; read again before
  `write_manifest`: a change is a WARNING naming both and the manifest records `changed (setup X, end Y)`; `unknown` is a
  WARNING (baseline.sh already stops on any WARNING in the capture log).
- capture.sh, ours: next to `warn_golden_notifs`, a WARNING when this build's golden records another `torch`, or none.
- `manifest_torch` (as `manifest_notifs`): `d.get('torch', d.get('setup',{}).get('torch'))`.
- baseline.sh, after the notification-set gate, `fail_b` (exit 1, no table) when the golden records no torch or an
  invalid one; ours' capture records none ("made before B-063: recapture"); or the two differ (both named; remedy: a cold
  boot until `dumpsys media.camera` matches, or a golden recaptured on this boot; names the Flashlight tile). Valid: `none`
  or a comma list of facings.
- golden.sh: capture reads `torch` from the staging capture's manifest, refuses a missing or invalid one (exit 1, raw
  captures kept), writes `"torch": "X",` after `"notifications"`; verify FAILs a missing or invalid one and prints "torch at
  capture: X"; `--against` FAILs when they differ but still compares every picture's md5.
- Backfill `tools/fidelity/golden/CP41.260828.004.A7/manifest.json`: `"torch": "none"` and a `"torch_note"` with the
  evidence above. PNGs, md5s and `total_bytes` untouched.
- Docs: FIDELITY_BASELINE (baseline 2 holds torch `none`; the B-061 re-run holds `front` and would not pass the gate; the
  cause in one line); probe note "o." in capture.sh's header with the dump form; ROADMAP B-063 fixed once the gates pass;
  DECISIONS D-046.

Do not: app code (`Controls.kt`, B-059, B-065); an override flag; a golden per torch state; `selfdiff.sh`; recomputing
baseline 2.

## Steps

1. `torch_state`, `manifest_torch`, probe note o; check live, read-only, on the emulator (today: `front`).
2. capture.sh: setup and pre-manifest reads, the setup field, the warnings. Fake adb: a `media.camera:*` case in the fake
   `dumpsys`, default a two-camera dump with both flashes false (`none`); switch file `$FAKE_STATE/camera` = `front`,
   `unknown` or `flip` (`none` on the first read, `front` after).
3. baseline.sh: the torch gate after the notification gate.
4. golden.sh: capture writes and refuses; verify and `--against` check.
5. Fixtures that hand-write manifests carry a torch (no assertion weakened): `sc_baseline_gate`, `sc_golden` g1/g4,
   `sc_golden_against` g5, `sc_notifwarn`; new scenarios below.
6. Backfill the golden manifest; `golden.sh verify` on it.
7. Optional live evidence: `capture.sh --side stock --state all --theme both` on today's boot, then `golden.sh verify
   --against` it: expected torch FAIL (`front` against `none`) with the 8 pictures identical (stock ignores the front
   flash). Record the result either way.
8. Docs and ROADMAP; `scripts/check.sh` green.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC1: fake default dump gives `"torch": "none"` in a capture's `setup`, the `front` dump `"front"`, both sides; no new MUT
  line in adb.log.
- AC2: `unknown` dump: exit 0, a WARNING naming the torch, manifest `unknown`; `flip`: a WARNING naming both, manifest
  `changed (setup none, end front)`.
- AC3: ours-side capture whose torch differs from the golden's: a WARNING with both values, never a failure on its own.
- AC4: `baseline.sh --ours-from` exits 1 with no table and a message naming both sides when ours `front` / golden `none`;
  golden without torch; ours without torch; either side `unknown` or `changed (...)`. Equal valid values pass the gate and
  stop at "ours/light/quick.png is missing" as today.
- AC5: `golden.sh capture` on the fake writes `"torch": "none"`; refuses (exit 1, no golden folder) on `unknown`.
- AC6: `golden.sh verify` FAILs a manifest without `torch`; `--against` with another torch FAILs and still prints per-picture
  identical/different.
- AC7: `golden.sh verify` on the committed golden passes and prints "torch at capture: none"; `git diff` touches only the
  manifest's two added fields.
- AC8: live, `torch_state` prints what `dumpsys media.camera` implies (today `front`); quoted in probe note o.
- AC9: `scripts/check.sh` green; every existing fidelity scenario passes, no assertion weakened.

Tests: `sc_torch_record` (AC1), `sc_torch_warn` (AC2, AC3), `sc_baseline_gate` extended (AC4), `sc_golden` /
`sc_golden_against` extended (AC5, AC6), a parser check on the real dump form (a fixture in `tools/tests/fixtures/` or the
fake dumpsys with exactly those lines).

## Decision

D-046 (agent): the harness records per capture and per golden which camera facings report a flash unit (`torch`, from
`dumpsys media.camera`); the baseline compares ours with the golden only when they are equal, with no override; the
committed golden is backfilled `none` from evidence (same boot as baseline 2's ours, which hid the tile), metadata only.
A boot with another value blocks the baseline until a cold boot matches or the golden is recaptured; revisit if frequent
(B-065 removes the cause on ours' side). Alternatives: masking the tile (blind to B-059); a golden per torch state;
gating on the back camera only (encodes the apps' code into the harness).
